Everything You Need to Specify the Right Gripper. Nothing You Don't.

A structured specification workflow built for engineers who need to compare actuation technologies, validate parameters, and produce a defensible shortlist — without wading through vendor datasheets one at a time.

Pneumatic, electric, vacuum, magnetic, soft-robotic, and adhesive grippers indexed in a single searchable catalog with consistent specification fields across all actuation types. Filter by payload (N), stroke (mm), IP rating, operating temperature, cycle time (ms), and mounting interface — using the same criteria regardless of technology. No switching between manufacturer microsites to compare apples with oranges.

Define your application constraints — workpiece geometry, surface condition, payload, cycle rate, environmental exposure, robot interface — and MatchMatrix scores the catalog against your requirements using a documented, auditable methodology. Every score is traceable to a specific criterion weighting. The methodology documentation is publicly available so you can verify the logic before you rely on the output.

Calculate required gripping force from part mass, acceleration profile, and safety factor. Estimate vacuum flow demand from cup diameter and cycle rate. Size pneumatic actuator bore from required jaw force and supply pressure. Calculators are embedded in the specification workflow — run them inline before passing results to MatchMatrix, so your inputs reflect real operating conditions rather than datasheet maximums.

Catalog entries distinguish between manufacturer-rated performance and independently documented operating conditions where data is available. Grip force figures are flagged by test configuration. Cycle time ratings are annotated with stroke distance and load assumptions. Where manufacturer data is the only available source, that is stated explicitly, along with the last-verified date.

Head-to-head comparison views let you place two or more grippers — across different actuation technologies — on the same specification matrix. Trade-offs are presented as data, not conclusions: where pneumatic outperforms electric on cycle time but electric outperforms pneumatic on positional repeatability, both figures are shown with their test conditions so you can weight them against your application's actual priorities.

Export your filtered shortlist as a structured specification document — including the MatchMatrix scores, the parameter inputs used, and the filter criteria applied. The output is formatted to attach to an internal design review or RFQ package. Every shortlist is reproducible: the same inputs will produce the same scores, so colleagues can verify or challenge the selection logic.

Structured technical content organised by actuation technology and industry vertical — automotive assembly, electronics handling, food and beverage packaging, logistics sortation. Technology deep-dives cover operating principles, specification parameters, advantages, limitations, and failure modes. Every article is written to advance a specification decision, not to introduce a concept. Articles link forward into the catalog and calculators at the point where the decision criterion appears.
