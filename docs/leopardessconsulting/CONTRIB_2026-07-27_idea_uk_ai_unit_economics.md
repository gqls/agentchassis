We measured what it costs to produce a real £29 product on current frontier models.

calls** — the other three were not logged, so the true total is higher but was not
captured on that run.

## The angle that needs no arithmetic

The strongest material here isn't the number, it's the demonstrable behaviour: the
pipeline **declined to pad the report** when it judged the further ideas it found
weren't good enough, and told the customer the paying demand was on the seller side —
unflattering, and against our own sale. For a consultancy selling judgement about AI,
"we built one that says no" is a better line than any cost figure, and it is evidenced
by the artefact rather than by a calculation someone can dispute.
