## Design-lead assessment (summary)

The deciding factor is small-size survival (a favicon is 16px). **Solid-fill marks
(c2, c3, c6) survive; line-art marks (c1, c4, c5) dissolve.** Recommendation: **c2**
(solid, spots as negative space) as the permanent mark — one shape, reverses cleanly,
unmistakably a leopardess at any size. c3 is the strong alternative and also gives us a
repeatable spot-texture motif for the wider site. Owner to choose (RUNBOOK H4).

Once chosen, the winning prompt becomes the `prompt` field of the `site_plan_imagery`
logo row, so the permanent asset is generated by the pipeline, not by this off-platform
preview step.
