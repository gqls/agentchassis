# SUMMARY — 2026-08-27 — the critic exists, and every page that matters has its own face

**What we're trying to do.** Make leopardessconsulting.co.uk an honest, distinctive shopfront
for the platform that builds it — copy that traces to evidence, imagery that distinguishes
rather than decorates, and automated quality machinery that a person can actually trust — while
fixing, at platform level, the faults this site keeps surfacing first.

**Where we've come from.** By the 25th the site was voice-clean but visually repetitive
(twenty-one pages opened with one photograph), the services page had been destroyed by
automation three times, the owner's contact button kept being rewritten to a calculator, and
the design critic the owner asked for in July had been proposed five times without being built.
On the 25th the owner answered four standing decisions in one sitting: drop the "trust" word
rule; give the dozen pages that matter their own heroes; this lane takes the platform fix for
the content destruction; this lane builds the critic.

**What we've done.** All four, plus what they uncovered. The trust rule is gone and the banned
phrases are off the live pages, verified there. Seventeen pages now open with their own
eyeballed hero — every generated image was looked at, four were rejected on sight and
regenerated. The services page is restored a fourth time and, with the home page's button and
showcase blocks, sits behind the platform's row-lock protection — a mechanism three earlier
restores never found, which reframed the bug (403) from "no guard exists" to "the guard is
undiscoverable and too coarse", with the finer-grained provenance design now ruled and shared
across two lanes. And the design critic is live end to end: granted storage through council,
seeded through council, dispatched through the work queue, and its first report is the real
thing — every finding concrete, the palette read correctly, and its lead complaint (the hero
sameness) the very defect being fixed while it wrote.

**Where we are now.** The critic's "after" run is in the queue; its report should drop the
sameness finding while keeping the rest — the discrimination test that separates a reviewer
from a random text generator. Five utility pages still share the old photograph, deliberately,
awaiting two archetype images. The critic's first report left eight concrete, actionable page
fixes nobody has started. The 403 field-level fix (the `__authored` marker) is designed and
ruled but not yet written. The design rotation restarted fleet-wide and will visit this site
within days — the first organic test of the row locks.

**Where we're going.** Read the after-run report and record the discrimination result; two
archetype heroes finish the imagery story; the critic's eight findings become the next visible
quality pass (several overlap the deferred carousel work); then the `__authored` marker goes
through council and the services-page class of damage becomes structurally impossible instead
of merely locked out.
