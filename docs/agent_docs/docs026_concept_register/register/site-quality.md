### SQ-004 — listing-class promise check — does a listing show the CLASS its heading promises?
- **status:** built and LIVE — detector `scripts/audit-listing-class-promise.py`, CronJob `listing-class-promise-check` applied 2026-08-31 17:05Z (schedule `25 7 * * *` UTC, suspend=false), first run verified at the POD's `exitCode=0` and at its `doc_notes` receipt (`subject_key='listing-class-promise'`, 17:06:53Z).
- **status-evidence:** `kubectl -n ai-persona-system get cronjob listing-class-promise-check -o jsonpath='{.spec.schedule}{.spec.suspend}'` → `25 7 * * * false`; manual job `lcp-verify-2` pod `lcp-verify-2-2qgk9` terminated exitCode 0; `SELECT created_at FROM doc_notes WHERE subject_key='listing-class-promise'`.
- **what:** The first fleet check that judges a listing against its OWN HEADING rather than against a schema. The rule: *a listing may show any content class its heading NAMES, and no class it does not.* It exists because the first paid customer build (boxingonline.com, 2026-08-31) headed its home page "Latest from the ring — fresh news, previews and results" over four explainer guides about the site's own tools, with every page validating `valid=true, issues=0` — no artefact was malformed, so nothing in the fleet could see it. Reads `page_components.content_data` (`articles`/`items`) plus the heading, preferring `content_data` and falling back to the first `<h1..h3>` of `rendered_html` — measured 2026-08-31, only **19 of 159** listing instances carry their heading in `content_data`, so the fallback is 88% of the corpus. Writes ONE `doc_notes` row per run **including clean runs**, so a missing row means the job did not run.
- **⚠ ASYMMETRIC BY DESIGN, and the asymmetry is the honest part:** it catches guides/tools appearing under an editorial promise; it CANNOT catch articles appearing under a guide promise, because `page_type` and `/blog/` are not content-class signals on this estate (measured 2026-08-31: guide-shaped pages are typed `blog-post` **246 across 30 sites** against `guide` **72 across 9**). The `page_type`-based rule the originating CONTRIB proposed is REFUTED and is kept as a runnable arm inside the detector so its zero stays a measurement.
- **escape clauses (each one pinned by a `--self-test` fixture taken from a live page):** a heading naming the class it lists is keeping its promise (robot-hands' "…& Integration Guides"); a heading naming no class promises nothing ("Start here"); the SUBTITLE is not part of the promise (4 of the first cut's 8 findings came from prose naming a tool in passing, and for the 139 markup-sourced listings "the first `<p>`" is an item's own excerpt); a class word inside the SITE'S OWN NAME is subject vocabulary, not a promise (garden-tools.uk, loancalculator.co.uk — suppressions are printed, never swallowed).
- **live finding at registration:** 1 — `leopardessconsulting.co.uk/blog.html`, "Latest Articles", 7 of 13 items are `/guides/tool-*-guide` pages. Found by the detector, not by a human.
- **sources:** `docs024_key_docs_latest/experience_loop/CONTRIB_2026-08-31_from_the_first_paid_build_four_experience_defects_that_every_check_passed.md` (defect 1); `docs024_key_docs_latest/experience_loop/NOTES_experience_loop.md` 2026-08-31 entry; `deployments/kustomize/services/listing-class-promise-check/`
- **relations:** experience_loop's promise-ledger family (the plan section this automates half of) · `no_horizontal_overflow`'s escape-clause precedent (`5042d5ecb`) — the lesson that a check without one reports correct pages as broken for ever · `bugs_open/419` (the zero-article plan that left the listing nothing to list) · SQ-003 design-critique-agent (the taste half; this is the promise half) · optional-key-budget-check (the CronJob shape copied byte-for-byte)
