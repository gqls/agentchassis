02 · How the method works

Generated across four lenses. Filtered hard. Verified.

01

Frame the audience honestly

We challenge the stated audience first — is this the right one, or is a better-fit audience being missed? Audience-fit is the single biggest predictor of whether the rest of the report has any value.

02

Generate across four lenses

Not one pass — four. What the audience deeply wants. Where generalist AI fails them. What just became possible in the last six months. What "done" looks like for them. Crossed with your assets and the current capability list.

03

Cut against the real alternative

For each candidate we name the specific free thing a customer would actually do instead. If that gets them most of the way, the candidate dies here. Most should. This step uses a different model from the generator, so it isn't marking its own work.

04

Verify, score, and rank

The survivors get checked with web research — does the data feed exist and what does it cost; do competitors already do it; is the willingness to pay real, not assumed. Then five-factor scoring, ranked output, cheapest test attached to each advancing candidate.

03 · Why it isn't another AI brainstorm

Most AI idea outputs are plausible-sounding things a free chatbot will say with confidence. We filter for ideas that survive a second model's critique and real verification — so what you get is closer to a research brief than a brainstorm.

Multi-model. Generation, critique, and verification use different providers — so the method doesn't rubber-stamp itself.

Capability-current. The lens of "what just became possible" runs against a watchlist we maintain, not the model's stale self-knowledge.

Honest results. We've delivered "no candidate advances" reports. If your space doesn't have one yet, we'll say so — and tell you what would change that.
