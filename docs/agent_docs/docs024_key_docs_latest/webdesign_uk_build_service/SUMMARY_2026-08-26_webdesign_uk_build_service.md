# SUMMARY 2026-08-26 — launch-ready: everything built and verified, one click remains

## What we're trying to do

Sell starter websites, built by AI, at webdesign.uk for £149 paid up front. The buyer is
an experienced web designer or anyone comfortable taking a folder of files, hosting it
and editing it: they get a ZIP that is theirs to host, edit and maintain, plus the site
live at our address for 30 days. One build, no changes, no refunds, said plainly before
the buy button. Any sort of site within the content limits — tools, guides, directories,
portfolios, community sites, not just businesses — but never an online shop that takes
payment. We are not a hosting company, and the copy says so.

## Where we've come from

Yesterday's summary recorded a deliberate pause: the owner wanted a copy and design
revision first, and had two big product questions open (his own edits before a customer
sees a site; customers editing their sites in the preview month). Since then the copy
brief arrived and was executed in full, both product questions were ruled, and the
launch unfroze. The repositioning was harder than it looked: the hero lives in the
site's positioning specs, not the writer's style rules, and it took finding that layer —
plus one credits outage and a queue stall worked around with a proven direct-dispatch
recipe — to get all seven pages saying the new things.

## What we've done

The whole site now carries the owner's position, verified served and stored: the
audience gate opens the hero, "No changes are included" stands as a full subtitle on
the home page and What-you-get and as its own heading on How-it-works, the categories
and the hosting truth are on the page, the editor guidance uses the owner's exact
wording, and every retired phrase and banned shape is at zero. The "Not active yet"
label is up, and it now survives the things that used to eat it. The customer door is
proven from the internet end to end: the second-click confirmation page renders and
answers through the new delivery-only listener, with the box repointed and every
control passing. Guards were armed as the rulings landed: the approval-language ban
(proven both ways — the known evader blocked, the denials passing) so the owner's now-
real internal edit step can never leak into copy; the discretionary-refund mechanism
decided (Stripe webhook-as-truth, entirely unadvertised); the analytics tag made
durable and its customer-site default ruled (owner's tag on hosted copies only, ZIP
always clean). And the domain service went from idea to proven chain in a day: generic-
only domain finding (brand names forbidden, shortest first, hyphens as last resort,
checked live at Nominet), registration dry-run-proven under the owner's own TAG,
Cloudflare zone-and-pointing scripted against a recipe already proven on a real zone —
all inside an opt-in layer that defaults to our ugg2.com subdomains, so dropping the
whole programme is a mode flip, not a rebuild.

## Where we are now

The site is finished, correct, guarded and parked. The one action between it and being
live is the owner removing the Cloudflare parking rule; verification after that is
minutes. Nothing can yet be sold — that is deliberate and separate: money needs the
Stripe keys, then the delivery email (its precondition layer is live and hardened; its
copy is now unblocked), then the refund consumer. Zero customers, zero tokens, zero
risk held anywhere.
