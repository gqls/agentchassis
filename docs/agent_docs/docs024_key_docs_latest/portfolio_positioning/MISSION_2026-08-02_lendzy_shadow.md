Build lendzy.co.uk as the borrower's FCA rulebook: a consumer-champion site for people
taking out or already holding high-cost, small-sum, urgent credit in the UK — payday and
other high-cost short-term credit, doorstep loans, rent-to-own, logbook loans, guarantor
loans. The reader is often already borrowing, urgent, and judged everywhere else; the
register is protective, zero judgement, urgent-friendly, short answers first. The site is
explicitly pro-regulator: a civilian champion of the FCA rulebook. Readers who know the
caps and complain are how the rules bite.

Editorial mode: rights-first guides plus rule-checking tools. The guides cover, at
minimum: the HCSTC price cap (CONC 5A: interest and fees capped at 0.8 percent per day,
default fees capped at 15 pounds, total cost never more than 100 percent of the amount
borrowed), affordability duties (CONC 5.2A), continuous payment authority limits (two
failed attempts, no partial takes), the two-rollover limit, complaining to the lender and
the Financial Ombudsman (DISP: 8 weeks for the final response, then 6 months to go to the
FOS), checking a lender on the FS Register including clone-firm warnings, unauthorised
lending being unenforceable (FSMA), Breathing Space (60 days, England and Wales), credit
unions as the capped alternative (3 percent per month, about 42.6 percent APR), and
reporting loan sharks (England 0300 555 2222, Wales 0300 123 3311, Scotland
0800 074 0878). Every regulatory figure must be quoted together with the named rule it
comes from and a pointer to check the source. Market rates, product rates and best-buy
numbers must never appear anywhere on the site: regulatory constants with rule names are
the only sanctioned numbers.

Interactive tools, each doing real calculation in the page: a price cap checker (given
amount and days, the maximum lawful initial cost is amount times 0.008 times days, the
default fee limit is 15 pounds, and the total ceiling equals the amount borrowed), a true
cost calculator (converts a quoted daily or monthly charge into an equivalent annual rate
and compares it with the credit union cap), and a complaint deadline calculator (8 weeks
from complaint for the lender's final response, then 6 months to reach the Ombudsman).

Navigation should be task-labelled, not generic: Check your loan (the tools), Your rights
(the guides), and Free help now, which links directly to the single most urgent page —
what to do if you cannot pay — not to a hub. Every page's chrome states plainly that the
site does not lend, never will, takes no applications, does no lead generation, and is
independent of and not affiliated with the Financial Conduct Authority. That independence
line is load-bearing and must appear on every page. Somewhere on the homepage carry the
exact phrase: know the rules before you borrow.

Compliance boundaries: not a lender, not a broker, no applications, no lead generation,
no product recommendations. If the reader's question is which product to choose, link out
or decline; if the question is what lenders are allowed to do to them, it lives here.
