# SUMMARY — 2026-09-02: the first four remakes are live

*Written to be read aloud. The previous read-out is
`SUMMARY_2026-08-25_sitemaps_became_a_mechanism.md`.*

## What we're trying to do

Turn the portfolio into a fleet of genuinely different, framework-built sites — starting by
remaking the 22 old hosted sites the owner cleared for rebuild, so that every site on the
estate is something the pipeline made, can audit, and can rebuild.

## Where we've come from

A week ago the remake programme was paused at its human gate: one real brief (advertise) held
for the owner's review, the cluster question unanswered, and nothing built. The machinery had
been proved on test domains but no old site had been remade.

## What we've done

Today, in one day: the owner said "go build advertise", answered the cluster question, and
approved the three cluster briefs — and all four sites went through the entire pipeline:
research, strategy, planning, tools, pages, imagery, rerenders, deploy. Advertise alone got
seven working interactive tools and about twenty-four pages, including the glossary and news
feed the owner's fullness edit asked for. The owner then moved the domains to Cloudflare, and
tonight all four serve their new sites to the public — the old Drupal-era pages are gone.

The day also stress-tested the machinery and it held: a credit outage paused everything and
the queue resumed itself; a placeholder phrase and a malformed component were both caught by
the platform's own validators before anything served; every stall we found was fixed at the
class, not the instance (site rows created for briefs now carry everything a release needs).
Alongside the builds, the register gained a games family, the sibling-site naming conflict the
owner flagged was resolved and executed by its own new session, and two new acquisitions were
recorded.

## Where we are now

Four of the twenty-two remakes are live: advertise.co.uk, websitepromotion.co.uk,
seotools.co.uk, designblog.co.uk. Their convergence tails are still draining (a few rerenders,
seotools' seven tools arrive via the discovery sweep's rotation, sitemaps generate on the next
selection). The owner's review queue holds the two test briefs, one style-phrase question on
advertise, and a fleet-wide backlog of claims-review items across eight sites that predates
today. Eighteen remakes remain, including the twin pairs that need owner calls and the
insurance site that deliberately goes last.

## Where we're going

Next: verify the convergence tails settle (tools on seotools, sitemaps everywhere), batch-check
the CTA items against served pages, then fire the next briefs — the remaining single-pagers
first, per the standing sequencing. The pattern is now proven end-to-end: brief → owner word →
released build → live site, inside a day.
