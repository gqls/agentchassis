# CONTRIB (from the "nominet page" session, 2026-08-17) — owner pricing statement

While settling the webdesign.co.uk Nominet registrar page, the owner stated a
pricing intent that belongs to THIS lane, not that page:

> "I'd like to charge £150 pounds as a transfer away fee. Basically they are
> buying the domain off me."

Context: in the build-service model the domain is registered by us and a
departing customer **buys the domain off us for £150**. This is distinct from
the direct domain-registration offering on webdesign.co.uk, whose Nominet page
truthfully states transfer-away is FREE (owner-confirmed, same exchange —
webdesign_couk PLAN §D16).

Nominet compliance note carried over from that work: the RRA's Key Terms
definition permits a transfer-away/termination charge **only if disclosed to the
customer in advance** ("what charges, if any, you make where Your Customer is
transferring their domain names to a new Registrar, or is otherwise terminating
their contract"). If this lane adopts the £150 fee, it must appear in the offer
terms the customer sees before purchase — not only in an internal price list.
Interacts with the "customer-owned DNS" line in the 08-11 offer: buying the
domain off us and customer-owned DNS need reconciling in the copy.
