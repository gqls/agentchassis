# SUMMARY — Phase 0 proven: the drain works end to end (2026-08-04)

**What we're trying to do.** Give the estate two standing faculties: a vigilant senior
designer that continually improves every site from whole-site structure down to the
finest detail, and an offer analyser that keeps asking whether each site answers its
target market's actual need profitably. Both built as checkers and handlers so
improvement is the system's normal behaviour. The organising rule: every detector ships
with its drain, because this platform's proven failure is correct findings nobody
consumes.

**Where we've come from.** The owner approved the two-programme plan on 2 August and made
four calls: manual per-site triggers, designer first, trial both Gemini and Claude as the
critic's eyes, broad autonomy with two exceptions. The first session shipped the
plumbing: two live config changes (the sweep re-pointed at the working scheduler lane,
still switched off; the dishonest three-audits-then-"clean" counter replaced by an honest
convergence gate) and three code pieces (the browser-measurement writer, whole-site
screenshot capture, the vision seam for both AI providers). Two of the three came back
from review with one blocking objection each.

**What we've done.** Both objections were answered and both revisions approved. Each
taught us something real: our own notes had claimed the wrong reader for parked findings
(logged as a wrong call), and one reviewer had been misled by our own concept register
still describing a bug as unfixed two weeks after it was fixed — the register is
corrected and a trap note now warns future readers. The code shipped in fresh images and
was verified in the running binaries, both positively and by the absence of a phrase the
revision removed. The audit workflow gained its write step. Then the proof: one
hand-fired sweep at a real site (its queue first reviewed by hand, five stale findings
cancelled with evidence). The gate assessed the site correctly, audited it, promoted
twenty-two findings, and the drain cleared them both ways — a two-week-dead finding
closed by the system observing it healthy, and a fresh one worked to completion with the
site's shared header visibly re-rendered and a nineteen-page refresh draining behind it.
That witnessed run closed the audit-counter bug for good.

**Where we are now.** Phase 0 and Phase 1 are complete, reviewed, live, and proven on a
real site. The improvement loop now audits honestly, promotes on every path, and its
findings reach workers that change pages. The browser-measurement audit and the vision
seam are live in the binaries but not yet exercised: the render audit runs as its own
job, separate from the sweep — a correction to our earlier understanding — and its first
witnessed run is the next small step. Nothing is enabled always-on anywhere; every
trigger remains the owner's or a session's deliberate act.

**Where we're going.** First the render-audit witnessed run: a browser measurement (a
contrast failure, a broken image) becoming a routed work item — which will also be the
first work item ever received by the CSS patch agent. Then the critic itself:
screenshots in, concrete routed findings out, the Gemini-versus-Claude comparison
recorded as a ruling. Behind it in order: the page-recompose handler, the revived
brief-fidelity audit, the anti-brochure planner constraints, and — on the same rails —
the offer and benefit analyser.
