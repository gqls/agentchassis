## What we're trying to do

Keep every site answering one question for ever: does this site actually serve its target
market's need, in a way that pays us — and does each page lead with what the reader gains,
rather than with what we want to say about ourselves? The offer analyser is the instrument;
the improvement loop is the delivery mechanism that makes its findings happen without a human
pushing each one.

## Where we've come from

Yesterday the analyser existed and was proven by two hand-fired runs on friendly-sized sites.
Enrolment in the automatic sweep was written but deliberately held — the owner's call to make,
priced in the held migration's own header. The worst-case test (our largest site) was owed. The
next track — checking the truth of the premise records the analyser grades against — was
approved in principle but had no file.

## What we've done (today)

- **The owner called enrolment: go.** Migration 409 applied, recorded, and the single-owner
  promotion audit stayed clean. Every improvement sweep now runs the offer analysis on any
  audit-due site, between the strategic review and the audit pass, with a failure arm that
  cannot strand a sweep.
- **The scale proof passed.** webdesign.co.uk — 104 reachable pages, the estate's biggest
  surface — completed in 51 seconds with no truncation, wrote a full ranked ordering, and filed
  four findings that each check out. The size worry the sizing raised on 08-14 is settled for
  v1: the worst case fits.
- **The claims-audit track is filed** (`features_open/034`): nothing on the estate checks the
  truth of a premise record, the analyser now grades against those records fleet-wide, and the
  file carries the motivating fabrication, the design constraints already learned, and the 13
  never-checked premises as its first, genuinely disconfirmable population.
- **A sweep was hand-fired at gaswholesalers** to witness the first sweep-driven cycle — the
  enrolled wiring exercising itself, and the five waiting findings travelling. Result in NOTES.

## Where we are now

The analyser is no longer an experiment. It is enrolled platform machinery: three of 22 sites
carry the ranked ordering artefact, fourteen findings are queued into live handlers, and the
sweep grows both numbers on its own from here. What is genuinely new information still to
gather: what handlers DO with the two finding classes the analyser can only hypothesise about
(page-body claims it cannot see), and whether the ordering artefact gains its first automated
reader (copy_quality_two_stage has the envelope).

## Where we're going

The claims-audit extension is the next build: without it, the analyser grades sites against
prose nobody has verified, and the leopardess case proved that prose can be flatly false while
passing every screen we have. Design questions are in 034, one owner-gated. The analyser's v2
(a bounded per-page excerpt so page-level findings become observations, plus a one-line
attribution fix now known to be intermittent rather than systematic) batches both changes with
one re-proof run on webdesign.co.uk, whose no-truncation baseline is today's.
