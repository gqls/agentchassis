webdesign.co.uk is a free, no-signup toolbox and learning library for people who design
and build websites. It is the owner's consolidation of two existing sites he already runs
(website-design.com and websitedesign.com) into one home. Everything on it runs in the
visitor's own browser: nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on a server, no account is
ever required. That is the whole promise, and it should read as the site's character
rather than as a marketing claim.

WHAT IT CONTAINS. Sixty-three small single-purpose web tools (colour and contrast
utilities, CSS and layout generators, image and canvas tools, performance and code
helpers, security and accessibility checkers, prompt and AI-workflow builders) and around
thirty plain-English articles explaining the ideas behind them. There is a global search
box in the header covering every tool and article.

PAGES TO PLAN — READ THIS CAREFULLY. Plan ONLY the home page. Do not plan, propose,
rebuild or recompose any other page. The tools index, the learn index, the about page and
the entire library of tool and article pages are hand-authored and will be registered
separately by their owner; they already exist as finished HTML and must be left alone. A
plan that invents extra pages will be discarded and re-run, so keep the page list to one.

The home page is a calm dashboard, not a sales page: a short two-column hero (a sentence
of copy on the left saying what the site is, an image on the right), then a grid of
featured tool cards, then a grid of guide cards, then a quiet closing call to action
pointing at /tools/index.html. It links only to /tools/index.html, /learn/index.html and
/about/index.html — never to a page that does not exist yet.

NAVIGATION. Exactly three items in the header: Tools, Learn, About. No other nav groups.

DESIGN — THESE VALUES ARE FIXED AND MUST NOT BE RE-INVENTED. Warm minimalist, light only.
Background #f9f8f6 (soft warm pearl). Cards and panels pure white #ffffff. Primary muted
sage #5c6b5d, hover #4a574b. Secondary sage #8a9a86. Accent soft terracotta #d4a373. Text
#2b2b2b, never pure black. Muted text #717171. Borders #edece9. Corner radius 12px.
Shadows soft and warm: rgba(43,43,43,0.04) to rgba(43,43,43,0.08). Cards lift 4px on hover
with the shadow stepping up one level. The header is sticky and white. Typeface Inter for
everything readable, Fira Code as a small monospace accent only — never for body text.

There is NO dark mode and there must never be one. Avoid: dark backgrounds of any kind,
gradients, glassmorphism, neon or saturated startup palettes, cool grey neutrals (every
neutral here is warm), pure black, sharp corners, heavy borders, auto-advancing carousels
and autoplaying media.

TONE AND HONESTY. Plain, calm, technical, British English. No hype, no exclamation marks,
no growth-marketing voice. Do not invent statistics, testimonials, client logos, awards,
user counts, team members or case studies — there are none, and a fabricated one would
have to be removed. There is no pricing, no newsletter, no e-commerce and no contact form:
the site sells nothing and collects nothing.

AUDIENCE. Working designers, front-end developers and technically-minded founders who
want a specific small tool without installing anything or handing over an email address.
