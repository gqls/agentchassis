What you actually get

Systems built around your business and your data, not around one AI provider's product line. No vendor lock-in, no black boxes.

Whichever model does the job

Text, images, code, and reasoning each get handled by whichever model does that job best. We don't sell one AI model dressed up eight different ways, and we're not tied to any single provider's roadmap.

Your data stays yours

Private hosting, models fine-tuned on your own records, and in-browser tools that never touch a server are all options we build with. If your data can't leave the building, we design for that from the start.

Open models, tuned for you

We fine-tune open-weight models such as Llama, Mistral, and Phi using techniques like LoRA and DoRA, adapting them to your documents and your terminology instead of leaving you stuck inside one vendor's product.

You approve what matters

Every system we build has review gates you configure yourself. Automate the repetitive parts and keep a person checking the parts that carry risk. Nothing runs unsupervised unless you decide it should.

Gets better after launch

Our systems run automated quality sweeps between review cycles. The sites, data pipelines, and tools we build keep improving after handover, not just on the day they go live.

Checked against sources, not guessed

Research and content agents check facts against official sources before anything gets published, with attribution and a credibility score attached. That doesn't make the output foolproof, but it does mean you can see where a claim came from.

Why businesses work with us instead of building this alone

The right model for the job, not the only one we offer

We use whichever model does the job best. A fast, cheap model handles the routine tasks, a large reasoning model takes the harder ones, and some jobs are better handled by a fine-tuned open-weight model running on your own hardware. We're not tied to one provider, so you get the model that fits the task, not the model we happen to sell.

Privacy comes first, not as an afterthought

Your data doesn't need to leave your building to get value from AI. We build private LLM deployments, fine-tune models on your own records, and where it makes sense, run models in-browser so nothing is sent to a server at all. Ask us what running your own private AI setup would look like.

Open models, not a walled garden

We work with open-weight models like Llama, Mistral, and Phi, and fine-tune them on your specific domain with custom embeddings. That means no vendor lock-in and no dependency on a single company's pricing or roadmap. If a better open model comes along next year, you can move to it.

You stay in control of what gets automated

Automation should save you time, not hand over judgement you didn't agree to give up. We build configurable review gates so you decide what gets automated outright and what needs a human to sign off first. No black boxes, and no surprises in what the system decided while you weren't looking.

Systems that improve after we've left

Systems we build keep improving after launch, through automated quality sweeps that run between review cycles. Websites, data pipelines, and internal tools get better over time rather than being frozen at the point we handed them over.

Checked against sources, not just generated

Our research agents check facts against official sources before anything gets published, with source attribution and a credibility score attached. It's a mechanism for catching mistakes early, not a promise that every output is correct. You should still read what comes out before you act on it.

The work we do

We've split the work into six areas. Find the one that matches your problem, or get in touch if you're not sure which it is.

Automation Systems

Taking the manual work out of everyday processes

We build pipelines that take over the repetitive parts of a job: screening requests, generating quotes, reading documents, routing tasks to the right person. That frees your team from the admin eating their day, so they can spend it on the calls that actually need a person, rather than replacing them.

Agent Systems

Networks of AI that work a problem together

Some tasks are too large for one model to handle on its own. We design networks of AI agents that split a problem apart, work on the pieces in parallel, and report back. Ask us about systems where tens of thousands of agents pass information between each other to solve something a single model couldn't.

Model Training & Company Knowledge

Teaching AI your business, not the internet

An off-the-shelf model knows a lot about everything and almost nothing about you. We fine-tune models on your own data using methods like LoRA and DoRA, and build retrieval systems that let AI search your emails, contracts, and internal knowledge before it answers. It gets your business right because it's actually read your business.

Data Collection & Pipelines

Turning public records into usable answers

We build systems that pull structured information from thousands of websites, company registries, and regulatory filings. This suits anyone who needs financial or market data gathered at a scale a person couldn't manage by hand, and kept current without someone doing it manually every week.

Private Deployment & Infrastructure

AI that runs on your terms, not a vendor's

Some data shouldn't leave the building. We deploy models privately, including versions that run locally or directly in the browser, so nothing sensitive touches a third-party server. We also set up the agent frameworks underneath, so your team can run these systems in production rather than just look at a demo.

Strategy, Sites & Implementation

From first conversation to working system

Most companies don't need a lecture on AI. They need help finding where it actually fits in their own processes. We work through that with you and build the case for what's worth doing. Then we build it, including fast, well-made websites and documentation when that's part of the job.

Talk to us before you buy any AI system.

We'll tell you if AI is the right answer for what you're dealing with. If it isn't, we'll say so, and point you elsewhere.
