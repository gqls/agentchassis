How to Use the Fine-Tuning vs RAG vs Prompting Decision Guide

This guide walks you through the Fine-Tuning vs RAG vs Prompting Decision Guide, a tool that helps you work out which approach fits your business problem instead of the one a vendor happens to sell. Fine-Tuning vs RAG vs Prompting Decision Guide

Answer 7 short questions about your data, constraints and priorities. You'll get a ranked recommendation across fine-tuning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and advanced prompting, with honest trade-offs for each, not just the "winner."

1. How much proprietary example data do you have for training?
2. How often does the underlying knowledge or facts change?
3. What are your latency requirements?
4. How sensitive is the data involved?
5. What's your budget and timeline?
6. What's the core problem you're solving?
7. What in-house technical capacity do you have?
Please answer all 7 questions before submitting to get your recommendation.

Your ranked recommendation

    This guide gives directional advice based on your answers, and it isn't a guarantee. Many real-world systems combine approaches (e.g. RAG for current knowledge plus fine-tuning for tone). Treat the top result as a strong starting hypothesis worth validating against your actual data and constraints.
