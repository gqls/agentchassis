How We Work

The people behind the work

FineTuning is a small team. We don't have a large org chart or a department for every service. What we have is a tight group of practitioners who build these systems directly, without a layer of account managers between you and the people doing the work.

Peter Grenfell

Principal Consultant

30+ years building software — from running one of the world's busiest sports websites to senior engineering at Bumble to financial systems. Built this AI framework because practical AI should be accessible to every business, not just those with engineering teams. The system now runs dozens of specialised AI agents that handle research, content, design, data collection, and ongoing maintenance — so business owners can focus on their business.

Archivist

Head of Research

AI managing agent. Leads a team of research specialists that do the homework before anything gets written or published. Depending on what's needed, Archivist's team researches your industry from multiple sources, checks facts against official records, verifies business data, and builds a clear picture of what your customers need to know. Your content and data are grounded in real, verified information — not generic filler.

Sentinel

Head of Quality

AI managing agent. Runs a team of specialist checkers that continuously review everything we build and maintain — your website, your content, your tools, and any data we collect on your behalf. Each checker focuses on something different: is the content accurate, do the tools work, is the design consistent, is the data clean. When something needs attention, Sentinel routes it to the right place. You can review changes before they go live, or let routine fixes happen automatically.

Quartermaster

Head of Maintenance

AI managing agent. Keeps everything we've built improving over time instead of slowly going stale. Quartermaster coordinates a team of maintenance specialists — monitoring content freshness, tool health, data quality, and site structure. Each maintenance cycle leaves your systems measurably better than before. You decide what gets your approval and what's handled automatically.

What We Build

Our work falls across a handful of distinct areas. Most projects draw on more than one of them.

AI Automation Systems

Remove the manual work from repeatable processes

We design and deploy automation pipelines that handle the tasks your team does the same way every time. Request screening, automated quoting, document analysis, workflow orchestration. If it follows a pattern, it can usually be automated.

Intelligent Agent Systems

AI that can reason, coordinate, and act at scale

Some problems are too large or too variable for a single model. We build networks of AI agents that work together, share information, and solve complex tasks collaboratively. Systems can run tens of thousands of agents communicating in parallel.

Custom AI Model Training

Models trained on your domain, not the general web

We use parameter-efficient techniques including LoRA and DoRA to adapt modern AI models to your specific context. The result is a model that understands your terminology, your formats, and your business without the cost of training from scratch.

RAG Systems for Company Data

AI that searches and reasons over your internal knowledge

Retrieval-Augmented Generation lets AI work with your actual data rather than what it learned during training. Emails, documents, financial records, marketing material, knowledge bases. We build the pipelines that connect your information to the model.

Bulk Data Collection

Structured data from across the web, at scale

We build extraction systems that collect structured information from thousands of websites automatically. Business data, financial data, market data. The output is clean, structured, and ready to use.

Financial and Company Data Pipelines

Automated collection from public registries and filings

We build pipelines that gather and analyse financial data from public sources including company registries and regulatory filings. The data arrives structured and current, without manual retrieval.

Private AI Deployments

AI that runs on your infrastructure, not someone else's servers

We deploy AI systems that operate privately, including models that run locally or in-browser. Your data doesn't leave your environment. This also reduces ongoing usage costs compared to third-party API calls.

Agent Pipeline Infrastructure

The plumbing that lets AI agents run in production

We install and configure the agent frameworks and orchestration systems your organisation needs to run AI agents reliably. Not a demo, not a prototype. A production setup your team can operate.

Rapid Website Generation

Complex, high-quality static sites built in minutes

We generate static websites for company identity, marketing pages, landing pages, documentation, and presentations. The output is production-ready and typically delivered far faster than a conventional build.

AI Strategy & Implementation

From first question to working system

We work directly with companies that are curious about AI but haven't yet found a clear path in. We help identify where AI is likely to help, where it probably won't, and what a sensible first step looks like.

High-Volume Data Acquisition

Structured collection from thousands of sources

We design systems that gather structured information from websites at volume and on a schedule. Useful wherever you need to monitor, compare, or aggregate data from sources that don't offer an API.

How we work, and why it's different

We pick the best model for each task

We're not tied to one AI provider. Text, images, code, and reasoning each get the model that handles them best. That means you're not paying for a one-size-fits-all approach that quietly underperforms on half your workload.

Your data stays yours

We can host models privately, run fine-tuning on your data using methods like LoRA, and deploy systems that process information entirely in-browser without sending anything to an external server. Privacy isn't a checkbox here — it's a build decision made at the start.

No vendor lock-in

We work with open-weight models including Llama, Mistral, and Phi, adapted to your domain with custom embeddings. If a better model comes along, we can move to it. Your system isn't tied to any single provider's roadmap or pricing decisions.

You stay in the loop

Our systems include configurable review gates. You approve the decisions that need a human eye and automate the ones that don't. There's no black box running unsupervised — the level of oversight is something we set together at the start.

Systems that improve between check-ins

We build automated quality sweeps into the systems we deploy. Websites, data pipelines, and tools don't just sit static after launch — they get reviewed and improved on a schedule, without waiting for you to raise a ticket.

Facts checked before anything goes out

Our research agents verify information against official sources before it's published or passed downstream. Source attribution and credibility scoring are built into the workflow, not bolted on as an afterthought.
