How-To Guide

Work Out Whether Your Business Is Actually Ready for an AI Project

The AI Project Readiness Checker asks a short series of questions about your data, your processes and your team, then gives you a plain read on where the gaps are. It's built for operations managers and business owners weighing up a first AI project who want an honest steer before they spend money on one. The result is a starting point for a conversation, not a verdict, and it can be wrong, so treat it as one input alongside your own judgement.

Level: Beginner
Best for: Operations managers and business owners considering a first AI project
  1. 1

    Answer the readiness questions

    Work through each question about your data, processes and team, answering as things actually stand rather than how you'd like them to be.

  2. 2

    See where the checker places you

    Read the result alongside the reasoning behind it, so you understand which areas are pulling your readiness down and why.

  3. 3

    Decide what to do next

    Use the outcome to decide whether to start small, fix a specific gap first, or talk to someone about the project you have in mind.

AI Project Readiness Checker

Answer nine short questions about your data, team, infrastructure, and organisational readiness. You will get an honest readiness tier and a plain list of what needs sorting out before you engage anyone, us included.

1. Data availability
2. Team AI literacy
3. Existing infrastructure
4. Defined success metrics
5. Budget clarity
6. Internal buy-in
7. Problem definition
8. Timeline expectations
9. Prior AI attempts

Please answer all nine questions before continuing.
