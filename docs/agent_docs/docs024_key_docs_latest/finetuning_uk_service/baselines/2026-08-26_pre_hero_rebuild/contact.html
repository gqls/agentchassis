Questions Worth Asking Before Any Call

Do I need to understand AI to work with you?

No. You need only to know your own business. We handle the technical side and explain everything in plain language. Our job is to understand your problem first — the technology comes second. If we recommend something, we will tell you why in terms that make sense to you, not in terms that require a computer science degree. You will never be expected to know what a model is, how fine-tuning works, or what a vector database does. That is our concern, not yours.

What actually happens on a discovery call?

We spend around 30 to 45 minutes asking about your business — what you do, where the friction is, and what you have already tried. We are not running a sales script. We are trying to work out whether there is a genuine problem we can help with. At the end, we will give you a view of whether AI is likely to help, roughly what an approach might look like, and what it would cost to find out more. No pressure, no follow-up sequence, no proposal sent before you ask for one.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, and we will not pretend otherwise. A focused automation for a single workflow costs far less than a multi-agent system processing thousands of documents. What we can tell you is that we work with SMEs, not enterprise budgets, and we scope projects to match what makes financial sense for the size of the problem. On the discovery call we will give you a realistic range once we understand what we are dealing with. If the numbers do not stack up, we will say so.

How long does a typical project take?

Smaller projects — a focused automation, a RAG system over your internal documents, a data pipeline — typically take two to six weeks from kick-off to working system. More complex builds involving multiple agent systems or custom model training take longer. We will give you a realistic timeline before any work begins, and we do not start the clock until the scope is agreed. We would rather set a timeline and hit it than promise speed we cannot deliver.

What if AI is not the right answer for my business?

Then we will tell you that on the call. There are plenty of situations where AI adds cost and complexity without adding value — and we have no interest in building something that does not work. Our reputation depends on projects that actually solve problems, not on closing every conversation. If a simpler tool, a process change, or just better software would serve you better, we will say so. We would rather lose a project than deliver something that disappoints.

What happens to our data? Is it safe?

Your data stays yours. We do not use your business data to train models on your behalf without explicit agreement, and we never pass it to third-party AI providers without your knowledge. For organisations with stricter requirements, we can deploy models that run entirely within your own infrastructure — nothing leaves your environment. We treat data privacy as a genuine business concern, not a checkbox. If you have GDPR or regulatory obligations, we factor those in from the start, not as an afterthought.

Do you work with businesses that have no existing AI infrastructure?

Yes, and that is most of our clients. You do not need existing AI tools, a technical team, or any prior experience with automation. We assess what you have, work with it where possible, and build only what is needed. We have helped businesses go from no AI capability at all to running systems that handle meaningful volumes of work — without requiring them to hire a data scientist or change how their team operates day to day.
