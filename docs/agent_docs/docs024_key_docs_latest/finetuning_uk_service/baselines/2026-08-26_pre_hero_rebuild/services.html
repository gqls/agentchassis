Get Started

AI systems built to solve one specific problem in your business, not to impress you with what they can do

From automation pipelines that clear a backlog overnight to private AI systems that never send your data anywhere, we pick whichever approach actually fits the job. No single vendor, no single technique. Human review stays in the loop wherever you want it. If data privacy is the sticking point before any of this feels workable, our GDPR & AI Data Risk Self-Assessment tool is a reasonable place to start.

The systems we build, and the problems they solve

AI Automation Systems

If your team spends hours checking requests, calculating quotes, or reading through documents before anything can move forward, that work can usually be automated. We build automation pipelines, ordered sequences of automated steps, that handle request screening, automated quoting, document analysis, and workflow orchestration, meaning routing tasks to the right person or system at the right stage. You choose where a human still checks the output before it goes further.

Intelligent Agent Systems

Some problems are too large or too varied for one AI model to handle alone, sorting through thousands of documents while checking each one against several sources, for instance. We design networks of AI agents, individual AI systems that can analyse information and pass results to each other, so they divide the work and solve it together. These systems can run at a small scale, or, where the task justifies it, coordinate tens of thousands of agents working in parallel.

Custom AI Model Training

Off-the-shelf AI knows the internet in general. It does not know your industry's terminology, your documents, or how your business actually works. We fine-tune existing models on your data, using parameter-efficient techniques such as LoRA and DoRA that adjust a small part of a model rather than rebuilding it from scratch, so the result understands your domain without the cost of training something from zero.

RAG Systems for Company Data

When the answer to a question already sits somewhere in your emails, contracts, or knowledge base, but nobody has time to find it, that is a search problem rather than a knowledge problem. We deploy Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, which let AI search your own documents and reason over what it finds instead of guessing from general training. It works across emails, financial records, marketing material, and internal knowledge bases, and it stays inside infrastructure you control.

Bulk Data Collection

Gathering information from hundreds or thousands of websites by hand is slow, and most businesses give up before finishing. We build systems that extract structured data, business listings, pricing, financial figures, whatever the task calls for, across large numbers of sites automatically. You get the result in a usable format rather than a folder of screenshots.

Financial and Company Data Pipelines

Checking company registries and regulatory filings by hand, one company at a time, stops working once you need the information regularly. We build data pipelines that collect and analyse financial and company data directly from public sources such as registries and filings, then keep that information current without manual re-checking.

Private AI Deployments

Every time company data goes into a public AI tool, it leaves your control, and for a lot of businesses that is a real compliance concern rather than a hypothetical one. We deploy AI privately, including models that run on your own infrastructure or directly in a browser, so nothing leaves unless you choose it to. It also tends to reduce ongoing costs, since you are not paying per request to an outside provider. If you want a sense of where your own data handling stands before we talk, try our GDPR and AI data risk self-assessment.

Agent Pipeline Infrastructure

Running AI agents in production, rather than as a one-off demonstration, needs something behind it: a way to schedule tasks, pass information between agents, and handle failures when they happen. We install and configure agent frameworks and orchestration systems, the layer that decides which agent does what and when, so your team can run and maintain its own agents rather than depending on us for every change.

Rapid Website Generation

A company identity page, a campaign landing page, or a set of documentation should not take weeks to produce. We build complex, well-designed static websites, pages that load fast and need no server-side software, for company identity, marketing, landing pages, documentation, or presentations, usually within minutes rather than a full project cycle.

AI Strategy and Implementation

Plenty of businesses are curious about AI and unsure where to start, or have tried a tool that did not fit and are wary of trying again. We work alongside your team to move from that curiosity to a system that runs inside your operation, not a slide deck or a proof of concept that quietly disappears after the demo.

High-Volume Data Acquisition

Some tasks need structured information gathered from thousands of sites at once: supplier catalogues, competitor pricing, or regulatory listings, collated into a single dataset. We design systems that collect this automatically at that scale, built to keep working as the list of sites changes rather than breaking after the first update.

Which of this actually fits your problem

Most of the services listed above sound like variations on a theme: pipelines, models, data. The differences matter more than they look, because the wrong choice for your problem will waste months even if the technology works perfectly. So before we build anything, we work out which of these actually fits what you are trying to solve, and we say so plainly if none of them do.

That starts with the problem, not the technology. A repetitive admin task usually calls for an automation pipeline: a sequence of steps that used to need a person and now does not, though a review gate can sit at any point where you want a human to check the work before it goes further. A question your staff keep asking about internal policy or past client work usually calls for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), where the AI searches your own documents for an answer instead of guessing from what it learned on the internet. A task where the AI needs to sound like your industry rather than a generic assistant usually calls for fine-tuning, training a model on your own material using efficient methods such as LoRA or DoRA so the adjustment is quick and affordable rather than a full retrain.

We are not attached to one AI provider, and we do not pretend one model is right for every job. Open-weight models such as Llama, Mistral, or Phi get used where running things privately on your own infrastructure matters, commercial models get used where they are simply better at the task, and the decision is made on that basis rather than on which vendor we happen to prefer. Where your data is involved, whether that is emails, financial records, or client files, it stays under your control unless you decide otherwise. If you want a sense of where your own data handling stands before you talk to us, our GDPR and AI data risk self-assessment walks through the questions a compliance lead would ask, though it can only point you in a direction, not replace proper advice on your specific setup.

If you are not sure which of this applies to you, that is a perfectly normal place to start. See how we work through a project from first call to a running system, or get in touch and describe the problem in your own words. We will tell you plainly if AI is not the right answer.
