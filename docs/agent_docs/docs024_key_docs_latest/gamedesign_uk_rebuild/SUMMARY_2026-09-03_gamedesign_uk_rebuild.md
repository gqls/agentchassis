# SUMMARY — gamedesign.uk rebuild — 2026-09-03 (third milestone: the first build was wrong; the second is queued)

Read aloud. Current state only. The two summaries of 2026-09-02 stay as they were written — the
second of them said "closeable" and was wrong within the hour, which is exactly what the series is
for.

## What we're trying to do

Make gamedesign.uk a real site in its own right — the professional-practice side of a domain pair
whose other half, gamesdesign.co.uk, does free tools and guides — and this time make it feel like
what it is about: full of games, images and energy, written for people who make games. And close
the monitoring gaps that let a broken site sit unnoticed, twice.

## Where we've come from

Yesterday's first build went live in fifty minutes and passed every check, and the owner looked at
it and saw a games site with no games in it: one picture, an articles page with no articles that
explained its own brief, a hero over a missing file, copy like a legal journal. The cause was the
specification I wrote — the picture guide banned game imagery, the brief asked for restraint —
and the pipeline obeyed it exactly. The checkers, when run, saw everything the owner saw and filed
it in a mode that acts on nothing. The improvement loop then tried to fix the site by planting
the sibling's tools on it, because the tool-suggester reads a site's identity and never its brief.

## What we've done

Rewritten the brief and the picture guide for the vertical's temperature — big bold game art,
real released games named and described as a player sees them, playable illustrations inside
articles, a rule that no page may describe its own brief. Filed the major error as its own
category on the owner's errors list, with runnable checks, and as bug 446. Held the tool plant
reversibly and filed it as 447; set the site to a growth posture of "hold", the first of thirty-
nine sites to use a control that already existed. Found the wrong-hero defect was fleet-wide —
six components, over a hundred and fifty pages — and the components lane fixed it the same
night. Re-dispatched the build — and lost twelve hours learning that a re-submission on a live site
is, by design, a strategy refresh that stops before the plan. The rebuild chain is now enqueued on
purpose and waiting its turn behind older backlogs.

## Where we are now

The site the public sees is still yesterday's wrong one. The new brief, guide and strategy are in
place and the classifier has already turned "print journal" into "bold, game imagery, the
sensibility of a magazine". The briefing item that starts the actual rebuild is filed and unclaimed
as of 09:25. When it runs, the plan should carry real article pages and pictures by default, the
about and contact pages should get their own heroes for the first time, and every page will need
reading as a reader, not counting.

## Where we're going

Watch the item get claimed; read the plan against the checklist before pages are written; read the
built site aloud against the errors list. Report the served hero URLs to components, the held
growth items to the improvement-loop lane, the plan shape to the 444 session. Then the owner's
three open questions — the contact address, an author, a newsletter — and one from the
improvement-loop lane: should a brand-new site be born on hold rather than open. The lane closes
when the owner looks at the site and does not have to say it again.
