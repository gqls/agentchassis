# Roadmap brief for `oufe.com` — Phase 1

Drafted 2026-07-25. This is the `roadmap_brief` text for the Tier-3 submission.
The planner treats it as **authoritative**: it builds only the pages named here
and does not invent additional ones. Keeping Phase 1 small is deliberate —
`features_open/015` exists because expecting a new domain to arrive fully formed
is how new domains arrive broken.

**No figures appear here, for the same reason as the mission brief.**

---

Phase 1 builds a small, honest skeleton. Five pages, nothing more. The site earns
its next pages by having the first ones be genuinely good.

**index** — the home page. What OUFE is, who it is for, and what it does that a
news feed or a data terminal does not. A short explanation of the approach:
we take a live corporate battle apart and show the mechanism, and we let you test
the assumptions yourself. Point clearly at the cases section as the main body of
work. Do not list statistics, achievements, client counts, or any measure of scale
— this site is new and has none, and saying so by omission is correct.

**about** — what this publication is and the standard it holds itself to. The
useful content here is the editorial method: every figure is traceable to a
document we can name and date; where a figure is unverified we say so rather than
estimating; analysis of mechanism is not advice about investment. State plainly
that this is an independent research publication rather than a firm, and make no
claim about staff, history, clients, or credentials.

**cases** — the index of case studies. In Phase 1 it lists one, and the page
should read naturally as an index that expects to grow rather than as a page
apologising for being short. Explain what a case study is here: a living dossier
on a real restructuring, kept current as the situation moves, with the mechanism
explained and the arithmetic made testable.

**cases/thames-water** — the flagship dossier hub. In Phase 1 this is the hub
itself, not the full library beneath it: what the situation is, why it is a
significant piece of financial engineering, which mechanisms it demonstrates, and
an index that will fill out as the deep-dive articles are written. This page must
assert no figures except those the site holds with a verified source. Where the
detail is not yet verified, say what will be covered rather than guessing at the
substance. Its structure should tolerate growing to dozens of sub-articles.

**contact** — a plain contact page. No form fields beyond what is genuinely
handled, no promises about response times.

Legal pages — privacy, terms, and a disclaimer — are hand-written separately and
are not part of the generated build.

There is no news feed in Phase 1. There is no pricing page, no subscription
offer, no paid product, and no sign-up: nothing on this site may offer or imply a
purchase, because there is nothing to sell yet.

Later phases, recorded here for direction only and explicitly **not** to be built
now: the deep-dive articles beneath the Thames Water hub; a library of tactic
explainers covering uptier exchanges, dropdown transactions and double-dip
structures; further case dossiers; interactive tools attached to each; and only
after all of that has proved it can be kept current, anything that asks a reader
for money.
