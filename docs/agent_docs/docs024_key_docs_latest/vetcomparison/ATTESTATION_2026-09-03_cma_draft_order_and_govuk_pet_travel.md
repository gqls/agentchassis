## 3. gov.uk pet-travel facts (attested 2026-08-26 for the AHC guide, re-stated with sources)

- https://www.gov.uk/taking-your-pet-abroad/travelling-to-an-eu-country-or-northern-ireland —
  AHC needed for a pet dog/cat/ferret GB→EU (also assistance dogs); microchip before/with rabies
  vaccination; pet ≥12 weeks; wait ≥21 full days after a FIRST vaccination (no wait after a
  booster with no break in cover); a GB resident CANNOT use a pet passport to enter the EU even
  if EU-issued (pet may be refused entry; NI residents can); signed declaration not to sell or
  re-home abroad; max 5 pets in a private vehicle unless event/commercial.
- https://www.gov.uk/taking-your-pet-abroad/getting-an-animal-health-certificate — must be
  signed by an Official Veterinarian (OV); gov.uk advises checking your vet can issue AHCs and
  asking them to help find an OV; take proof of microchipping date + vaccination history;
  validity from issue: 10 days for EU entry (worked example: issued 1 Nov → enter up to and
  including 10 Nov), 6 months onward travel within the EU, 6 months re-entry to GB; a NEW
  certificate per GB→EU trip; one certificate covers onward travel between EU countries within
  the same trip.
- https://www.gov.uk/taking-your-pet-abroad/tapeworm-treatment-for-dogs — dogs travelling to
  Finland, Ireland, Malta, Northern Ireland (unless a Northern Ireland pet travel document is
  held) or Norway need tapeworm treatment (praziquantel or equivalent effective against
  Echinococcus multilocularis), given no less than 24 hours and no more than 5 days (120 hours)
  before arrival; also needed for the return to GB from most countries.

## 4. Known live-site discrepancy this attestation makes legible

The live guides state the £21 / £12.50 prescription caps as settled figures. The draft Order
defines them as bracketed placeholders — e.g. *"'Initial Primary Prescription Fee Cap' means
[£21 inclusive of VAT. This will be adjusted for inflation ... before the Order is made]"*
(NOTES 07-25 reading, re-confirmed against the 09-03 fetch). Recommended honest phrasing for any
repair (owner's call, per the content freeze): "the draft Order proposes a cap of £21, to be
finalised — with an inflation adjustment — when the Order is made."
