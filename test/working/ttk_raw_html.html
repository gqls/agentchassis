Time-To-Kill (TTK) Calculator

TTK is the heartbeat of your combat loop. A low TTK (e.g., 200ms in Call of Duty) emphasizes positioning and reflexes. A high TTK (e.g., 1.2s in Apex Legends) emphasizes tracking aim and team coordination.

Use this tool to find "Breakpoints". For example, if a weapon does 33 damage, it kills a 100HP target in 4 shots. Buffing it to 34 damage drops it to 3 shots—a massive power spike. Buffing it to 35 damage changes nothing. Know the difference.
